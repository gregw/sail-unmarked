Unmarked Racing

Virtual marks, real racing!
  • Virtual courses with no race committee on the water or marks to lay
  • Courses are always available for practice or record attempts
  • Circuit courses that can be started/finished at one of many lines. Boats from different clubs can race the same circuit at the same time but join/leave from their own local waters.
  • Distance corrected handicapping, where each boat is allocated a specific course length: all boats start together, and the first home wins on handicap.
