Unmarked Racing

Sail racing around virtual marks

Every mark is a line crossed by GPS rather than a buoy rounded, so a club can race with no marks laid and no committee boat on the water. A boat's phone detects and times its own crossings, with no network needed while it sails; this server hands out the courses before a race and collects the results after it.
